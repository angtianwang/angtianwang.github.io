## About Me

I am a Research Scientist at ByteDance, where I focus on developing high-quality, controllable, and temporally consistent video generation models. My work spans localized control of video synthesis, ID/IP-conditioned video generation, and long-form video generation, along with contributions to large-scale video foundation model training. Our research has been successfully deployed in TikTok effect products such as [AI Mermaid](https://www.tiktok.com/effect/AI-Mermaid-2386487441) (best AI effects on TikTok since 2023). Previously, I earned my Ph.D. in Computer Science from Johns Hopkins University, advised by Bloomberg Distinguished Professor Alan L. Yuille.

## Research Interests

- **3D Vision:** Object Pose Estimation, Reconstruction, Shape Estimation
- **Robust Vision:** Robust Detection and Classification, Robustness under Occlusion
